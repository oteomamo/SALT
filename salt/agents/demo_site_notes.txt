Site survey, Bristol Road
Visit 3, morning, clear weather.

The array is fourteen panels of 640 watts on the south face, so 8.96 kW
at the module level. The roof pitch is 38 degrees and there is no
shading from the oak until late afternoon in winter.

The inverter on site is a 5 kW continuous unit with a 10 kW surge
rating. It was installed four years ago and is out of warranty next
spring.

Metering from the last two winters shows an evening draw averaging
1.4 kW between six and eleven, rising to 2.1 kW on the coldest fifteen
evenings. The heat pump accounts for most of that rise. Overnight base
load with the heat pump cycling is close to 0.4 kW.

The quote on the table is for a 9 kWh lithium iron phosphate pack with
a usable depth of discharge of 90 percent. The installer has assumed a
five hour evening and has not costed the coldest evenings separately.

The homeowner wants to know whether the quoted pack covers a winter
evening without importing, and is willing to accept a small number of
nights where it does not.
